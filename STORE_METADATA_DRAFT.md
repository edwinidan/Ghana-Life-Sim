## Full Description

Create a character and live a full Ghanaian life, one year at a time.

In Ghana Life Sim, every age brings choices. Go through school, chase a career, try side hustles, build relationships, manage money, support family, start businesses, face health scares, and deal with the kind of drama that can only happen when life, love, pressure, and reputation all meet in one place.

Your decisions affect your stats, cash, debt, relationships, education, career, health, family, goals, and legacy. Some choices bring opportunity. Some bring wahala. Some bring both.

Play until the end of life, earn a legacy ribbon, unlock achievements, and start again to see a different story.

Features:

- Create your own character
- Age up year by year
- Make choices in Ghanaian life events
- Manage health, happiness, smarts, looks, reputation, discipline, street sense, and connections
- Handle cash, debt, school, career, side gigs, housing, business, relationships, family, goals, and achievements
- Reach death and receive a life score, ribbon, and legacy summary
- Restart and try a new life path

Ghana Life Sim is a fictional life simulation game inspired by Ghanaian culture, everyday choices, ambition, family pressure, love, hustle, and legacy.
